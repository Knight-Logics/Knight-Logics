How the Partnership works

You run your calls through Aria and your jobs through our ticketing system. Every call, ticket and invoice is tracked in one place, and you get a monthly report. On a 12-month term the setup fees for Aria and Ticketing are waived.

Questions

What does "call usage" mean for Aria?

Aria's phone minutes are billed at cost on top of the setup fee. Usage is passed through transparently: you see the same itemized minutes we're billed, with no markup hidden in a bundle.

What if I need more than the package covers?

We quote the extra scope as a flat price in writing before any work starts.

How do I pay?

We send a written scope and an invoice after the call. There's no checkout on this page. Packages are 50% deposit to start and 50% at delivery. The Partnership is billed monthly.

Can I cancel the Partnership?

After the 3-month minimum you can cancel any time with 30 days' written notice.
