Knight Group: from portal ticket to paid invoice in one record

Knight Group handles property-maintenance jobs that arrive as portal tickets. We built one dispatch system that runs each job from intake through field proof to a Stripe invoice.

The problem

  • Ticket details were copied into side systems
  • There was no required photo step before closeout
  • Billing lagged after jobs were finished
  • Payment status wasn't visible on the job screen

What we built

  • Ticket intake queue
  • Mobile job updates for field crews
  • Photo attachments for each job ID
  • Branded PDF completion reports
  • Stripe invoice or payment-link trigger after the office reviews the proof

This runs against real Knight Group maintenance jobs. It's an internal reference build, not a resold product.
