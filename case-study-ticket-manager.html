Ticket Manager: every job tracked from first call to paid

The Ticket Manager is the job-tracking system behind Knight Group dispatch. It holds each ticket, its status, the field photos, the PDF report and the invoice in one record.

What it does

  • Intake queue for portal, phone and form tickets
  • Job statuses through closeout
  • Required photo proof before closeout
  • PDF completion packets
  • Stripe invoice trigger

Where it runs

It runs daily for Knight Group. In Knight Command, the internal ops shell, Vendoroo / KG Dispatch tabs sit next to the CRM, referrals, email and social tools.
