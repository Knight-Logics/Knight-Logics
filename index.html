Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what Tampa Bay business owners ask most before getting started.

How much does a small business website cost?

Website Build is a flat $2,500 for up to 8 mobile-ready pages, with forms that feed your ticketing. The Full Ops Bundle ($7,500) adds Aria, Ticketing & Dispatch and one Automation Sprint. Every package has written deliverables and a set timeline. See the pricing page.

Do you offer local SEO services in Tampa Bay?

Yes. Knight Logics handles Google Business Profile optimization, schema markup, Search Console setup, sitemap submission, and on-page technical SEO for businesses across Tampa, Clearwater, St. Pete, and Safety Harbor.

How do hand-coded sites compare to WordPress or Wix?

Custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript gives Knight Logics control over performance, markup, schema, redirects, internal linking, and conversion design without a theme or plugin stack. That does not guarantee higher Google rankings — it removes builder lock-in and recurring plugin fees so the site stays lean and measurable.

Can you help my business show up on Google Maps?

Yes. Maps visibility comes from a properly optimized Google Business Profile combined with a well-structured site. Knight Logics handles both GBP setup, category selection, service area configuration, and the technical SEO that validates your local relevance.

Do you work with businesses outside of Tampa Bay?

Yes. Website design, automation, and software development services are available remotely across the United States. On-site collaboration is available for Tampa Bay and Pinellas County clients.
